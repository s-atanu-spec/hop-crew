Three buttons, one good read

Every rival tells you its move before it makes it. The skill is reading the banner and trusting your timing.

↑

Jump

Tap the scene, or press Space or Up. Tap again in the air for a double jump over the big ones.

↓

Duck

Hold Duck, or press Down or S, when something swoops in at head height.

⚡

Boost

Clean escapes charge it. When the cue turns green, press B and each runner does its own thing.

Every path is its own place

Courtyards, tea gardens, rooftops, a neon causeway, a relay post on Mars. Each has its own rivals, music, street life and a hidden side trail to find.

Deliver on time, earn the sticker

Each run starts with a handover and a delivery clock. Hits cost you time; clean escapes win it back.

  • On time earns that path's sticker for your book.
  • Find the three hidden parcels on the way and it turns gold.
  • Fill the book and your runner gets a crown, plus a sticker pack to download and keep.
  • Watch for the Red Dino. Dodge it, or find the star charm and send it packing.

Put it on your phone

It installs straight from the browser, opens full screen and keeps working with no signal.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome and press Play now.
  2. Open the menu (three dots) and choose "Add to Home screen".
  3. Choose Install. Hop Crew now has its own icon.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari and press Play now.
  2. Press the Share button.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen".

Private by design: no accounts, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere. Your progress stays on your own device. Details are in the terms and privacy notes.
