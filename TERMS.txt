Hop Crew 39
A side-on runner for phones and desktop.
Created by Atanu Sarkar. Made with Claude.


WHAT IT IS

Hop Crew is a running game. Pick a runner, read the rival, jump or duck, and reach the finish.

  Paths:  21 paths and 10 side trails, each with its own scenery, rivals and obstacles.
  Story:  every path is a delivery for the festival. An on-time delivery earns a sticker.
  Runners:  boy, girl, dino, robot and heroes, with outfits and colours.
  Extras:  a sticker book, a journey map, a local Champions Board and a sticker pack you can download.


HOW IT WORKS

Hop Crew is one web page, play.html, with the game, its pictures and its sounds inside it. It runs in a web browser, or inside an app that shows web pages. Nothing is installed on a computer. On a phone you may add it to the home screen; the phone then keeps a copy so it opens without internet.


PRIVACY

  - Hop Crew has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.
  - It collects nothing and sends nothing. After the page has loaded it does not use the internet.
  - Your progress, settings, stickers and scores are saved on your own device only, in the browser's storage for this page. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.
  - The Champions Board is local to your device.
  - The sticker pack is made on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share or send it yourself.
